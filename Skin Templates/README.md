# README
The textures and information provided here was created by [**Manikun**](https://thunderstore.io/c/atlyss/p/Manikun/) and taken from [**Sculptors Delight Pack**](https://thunderstore.io/c/atlyss/p/Manikun/Sculptors_Delight_Pack/). Permission to publish them here was obtained and as their CHANGELOG states, you are free to use them for your own purposes.

Basic things to note.
- Boundary: Where the texture for that part generally stops. Drawn in blue.
- Mirror: Where the texture is mirrored, usually on the same part. Drawn in black.
- Mirror (arrows): Arrows to help guide placement of textures. Also shows spots where the mirror doesn't line up.

Additional notes.
- Boundaries aren't perfect- I recommend coloring outside the lines a bit to prevent any issues!
- Proportion changes on the characters can cause warping and clipping. I recommend not using any complicated patterns or textures if you want to keep your seams clean.
- The in-game models have no overworld shading! Shading your model will keep the character's topography from looking flat.
- Hair/clothing will cover most seams, don't make yourself sick trying to blend them absolutely perfectly.
- Have fun!
